Product 01 · Banking

One balance across your Nigerian banks.

Stack is a Nigerian app that brings the bank accounts you already use into one place. You link accounts from different banks, see one combined balance, and pay or transfer from whichever account you choose.

  1. Link your accounts Connect the Nigerian bank accounts you actually use. Add another whenever you open one.
  2. Read one balance Stack adds them into a single combined balance, so you are not opening each bank on its own.
  3. Pay from the account you pick When you pay or transfer, you choose which linked account the money leaves from.

Your money stays in your bank accounts. Stack is where you see those accounts together and move money from them.
