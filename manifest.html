Product 03 · Publishing

An ebook and a sales page, without the blank page.

Manifest.AI is a web app for someone who wants to sell a digital product and does not yet have an idea, research, or writing. It takes that intention through to a finished ebook with a sales page.

  1. Start from the wish to sell You do not need a topic, an outline, or a draft. “I want to sell a digital product” is enough to begin.
  2. Idea, research, and writing Manifest.AI does that work. The book is written for you, aimed at something a person can actually sell.
  3. Leave with a page that sells it The result is a finished ebook and a sales page, so the product is ready to offer.

Manifest.AI is for the person who wants the finished product, not another empty document to fill in.
