Product 02 · Trading

A trading desk that never holds your money.

Stratum is a crypto trading desk built by Z Technologies. It finds trade setups, gives each one a confidence score, and either shows them to you or places them for you, within risk limits you set.

  1. Connect your own broker You use API keys that can read and trade, and cannot withdraw. The account stays yours.
  2. Set the risk limits Stratum only works inside the limits you choose. A setup outside those limits does not get placed.
  3. Review, or let it trade Each setup comes with a confidence score. You can look at them yourself, or have Stratum place them for you.

Stratum never holds your money. Keys that cannot withdraw can see the account and send orders. They cannot move funds out.
